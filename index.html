      The idea behind it
      
    
      Some data is useful only in the market where you collected it. Other data, like location or transaction records, can power businesses elsewhere. The theory predicts that firms whose data travels well may leave markets where they are doing fine, because the outside opportunities are worth more. Firms whose data is tied to one market may stay even when results are weak.
      
    
      Niantic is one illustration: it sold a profitable games business to build mapping tools on the location data those games produced.
      
  
      
  
      
    
      Help test it
      
    
      Researchers rarely see what data firms hold, so this theory has not been tested. Your firm may have exactly the information needed: what data you hold, where else it could be used, what limits its reuse, and which markets you have entered or left. If you would like to explore a research collaboration, contact details will be added after review.
