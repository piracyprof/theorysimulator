Inherited or new? The test this lineage makes possible

Because every model is stored as a specification file that names its parent, the tool can switch off whatever a child added and rerun its propositions. Predictions that survive are inherited; predictions that disappear are the child's own contribution.

Status: the focal model's specification is complete. Specifications for its ancestors, and the switch-off test, are planned for Stage 2.
